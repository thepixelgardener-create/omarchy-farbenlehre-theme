# Wallpaper generation

The wallpapers are AI-generated artistic interpretations of the repository's Goethe-inspired design, not scientific diagrams. The first three were created with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool, `04-daemmerung.webp` was made later in ChatGPT, and the prompts below are the ones actually used. An earlier round asked for "3840x2160" in the prompt text and was returned at 1672×941 regardless: the output size is fixed by the generator and cannot be prompted, so the prompts now ask for a 16:9 landscape and leave resolution out. The 4K files in `backgrounds/` were upscaled from these 1672×941 results afterwards; ASSETS.md describes how.

The fifth wallpaper took several rounds. Short prompts for a moonlit Brocken returned generic photographic winter landscapes. The prompt that worked takes its composition from Goethe's own sketch of the Brocken from his winter journey of 1777 (the long low summit, two dark forest bands, a wide snowfield) and spells out the light, the style and what to leave out.

The fourth prompt is deliberately short. Longer versions asked for one pencil casting a blue and an amber shadow, Goethe's coloured shadows, and the generator kept placing the shadows where the light could not put them. Leaving the composition to the generator gave a better picture.

The sixth prompt goes the other way: long, but it describes qualities, light and colours rather than placing objects, and it rules out everything that would make the form recognisable.

Deep chiaroscuro, extraordinary tonal range, luminous pigment and restrained but intensely saturated colour.

Strong horizontal rhythm, cinematic depth, vast negative space, asymmetric illumination from the upper left. **Mood:** Sublime, mysterious, solemn, contemplative. The overwhelming stillness of nature, illuminated for one fleeting moment. A feeling of eternity, solitude and quiet wonder.

## 06-werdende.webp

A 16:9 mystical oil painting in the tradition of 17th- and 18th-century Old Masters, exploring Gestalt, emergence, metamorphosis, and das Werdende — the eternal process of becoming.

Against a vast, nearly black void, an immense, mysterious organic formation emerges from darkness. It is neither plant, animal, mineral, nor architecture, yet somehow all of them at once. Its substance folds, curls, stretches, and flows into itself, forming enormous hollow arches, delicate translucent membranes, intricate branching filaments, and sweeping, wave-like structures.

The formation appears to be creating itself through its own movement. Nothing is assembled from separate objects; every curve grows naturally from another, every surface transforms into something new. The boundaries between matter and emptiness, structure and movement, chaos and order become indistinguishable.

Warm amber and antique gold illuminate thin, almost transparent edges from within, while subtle lavender, muted violet, and pale blue shimmer through deeper layers. Rich umber, burnt sienna, charcoal, and blue-black shadows dominate the composition. Dramatic chiaroscuro, delicate atmospheric transitions, luminous glazing, and richly textured oil brushwork evoke the mysterious grandeur of an undiscovered Old Master painting.

The composition is asymmetrical, expansive, and profoundly organic. Its central form is simultaneously recognizable and impossible to identify. It possesses an underlying harmony that emerges from the relationships between its parts rather than from symmetry or repetition.

The painting captures neither a beginning nor an end, but the living instant in which something becomes what it is. A visual meditation on emergence: the whole is more than the sum of its parts.

Mystical, sublime, contemplative, strange, and quietly overwhelming. Painterly rather than digital, with subtle canvas grain and centuries-old pigment depth.

No people, no faces, no recognizable creatures, no symbols, no text, no conventional scenery, no futuristic aesthetics, no geometric perfection, no obvious spirals, no decorative fantasy motifs. Let the form remain fundamentally ambiguous and alive.
